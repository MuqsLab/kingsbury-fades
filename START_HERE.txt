MAKE BOOKINGS LIVE (SETMORE)
----------------------------
1. Create or sign into YOUR Setmore account at https://www.setmore.com
2. Create staff profiles MUQ and AHMED. Set their working days/hours:
   Mon CLOSED / Tue-Sat 10 am–9 pm / Sun 10 am–6 pm (Melbourne timezone).
3. Create the exact 11 services from content/services.json, in AUD.
4. Apply a FIVE-MINUTE buffer to every service in Setmore. Buffer times are
   configured separately from customer-visible service durations.
5. Set Booking Page policies:
   - lead time: 30 minutes
   - scheduling window: 14 days
   - slot size: 15 minutes
   - cancel/reschedule notice: 2 hours
6. Booking flow: enable Any Team Member, and turn on Provide Multiple Services.
   Keep Skip Team Members off so people can choose Muq or Ahmed.
   TEST whether the Any Team Member and multi-service options work together
   in your account. The setting names could change.
7. Contact fields: Name required, Phone required; hide or make Email optional,
   and disable customer-login requirement if you want no email requirement.
8. Keep online payment collection / deposits OFF. Customers PAY IN STORE.
9. Use separate staff logins. Enter breaks/walk-ins from each barber's Setmore
   calendar so those slots cannot be double-booked. Manage no-shows manually.
10. Copy your OWN Booking Page URL, like https://yourshop.setmore.com
    (this is an illustration, not an actual shop address).
11. OPTIONAL: Add each barber’s direct Setmore team booking link in the
    Barber profiles editor so their Book buttons pre-select Muq/Ahmed. Without
    these, the buttons open the standard Setmore form.
12. In Pages CMS after setup, open Website settings and text,
    paste it into "Setmore Booking Page URL", then Save.
13. Reopen the public website and TEST a real booking on a phone.

IMPORTANT BOOKING LIMITATIONS
-----------------------------
Setmore is an external booking system. The website itself does not create or
store appointments; it embeds Setmore in a pop-up window.

These features were requested in our planning but are NOT yet implemented or
verified by this starter:
- One combined group booking of up to four PEOPLE with different barbers and
  synchronized times. Setmore's 'multiple services' feature is not the same
  as multi-person group booking. For now, customers can create separate
  appointments, and you should enable 'Book another appointment' on confirmation.
- Self-service cancel/reschedule using BOTH phone number + booking reference.
  Setmore offers booking IDs but its documented customer self-service uses
  customer login, which typically involves email. For phone-only clients,
  have them call the shop to change bookings until you choose another solution.
- Customizing the inside of the Setmore form to exactly match our own booking
  summary. The embedded booking form and confirmation are Setmore's UI.
- Automatic 'Add to Calendar' may depend on Setmore features; test it.
- Live Google review cards require owner authorization of a widget provider
  (RepU embed code). The public listing is already linked.
- Tracking no-shows in the website's content admin. No-shows must be handled in
  Setmore's appointment/calendar workflow.

TO EDIT WEBSITE FROM PHONE OR COMPUTER (FREE)
----------------------------------------------
We'll use a free, GitHub-based editor called PAGES CMS.

1. Create a free GitHub account at https://github.com
2. Create a new GitHub repository (e.g. kingsbury-fades-site).
3. Upload THE CONTENTS of this folder to the ROOT of the repository (ensure
   index.html is at the repository root, not hidden in another subfolder).
4. Open https://app.pagescms.org/ and sign in with GitHub.
5. Install/authorize Pages CMS access to that repository.
6. It automatically reads the included .pages.yml configuration.
7. Edit "Website settings and text", "Services and prices", "Barber profiles"
   and "Haircut gallery"; upload images in the media chooser.
8. Save. Pages CMS commits your changes to GitHub. Once your hosting has been
   connected to GitHub, the PUBLIC site updates automatically on deployment.

WHO CAN EDIT?
- ONLY the GitHub/Page CMS users that YOU authorize can change content.
- Muq and Ahmed should use Setmore's staff calendars for booking management;
  no need to grant them access to your website content repo.
- This edits text, prices, service listings, gallery, photos, hours, socials,
  links, etc. Significant page layout redesign still requires code changes.

MAKE WEBSITE PUBLIC FOR FREE (CLOUDFLARE PAGES)
-----------------------------------------------
1. Create a free Cloudflare account at https://dash.cloudflare.com/
2. Go to Workers & Pages > Create > Pages > Connect to Git.
3. Connect the GitHub repository you just created.
4. Framework preset: NONE (plain HTML website).
5. Production branch: main
6. Build command: exit 0 (or leave empty if permitted).
7. Build output directory: . (repository root, containing index.html).
8. Deploy. You'll receive a free *.pages.dev website address.
9. Return to Pages CMS to edit photos, text or prices on your phone.
10. Cloudflare automatically deploys changes from the GitHub main branch.

NOTE: Direct Upload to Cloudflare Pages is simpler for one-off publication but
DOES NOT provide automatic GitHub edits. Use CONNECT TO GIT for your goal.
The address kingsburyfades.pages.dev is just an example and is not reserved.

GOOGLE REVIEWS — REAL AND AUTOMATIC
-----------------------------------
Your exact Google Maps listing is already linked in the site. The Google Reviews
area has a feed slot but cannot show review cards until YOU create/authorize a
widget for your Google Business Profile. No reviews or ratings are invented.

Recommended $0 automatic option, RepU: https://www.repu.ai/
1. Sign in to RepU and connect Kingsbury Fades Barbershop's Google Business Profile.
2. Choose a free Google review carousel/widget and use the dark theme.
3. Copy the complete embed code it supplies (usually a div and script).
4. In Pages CMS > Website settings and text, paste it into
   'Automatic Google reviews widget embed code', then Save.
5. On your published site, genuine Google review cards will appear in the existing
   Reviews section and new ones will sync automatically on RepU's schedule.
6. Review the widget on mobile and keep a READ GOOGLE REVIEWS button for the
   full, up-to-date public listing. Free RepU shows Powered by RepU branding.

You do not need to manually paste a review each time a customer leaves one.
RepU says its free plan auto-syncs up to the latest 30 reviews, but new reviews
may take a while to appear; widget limits and terms can change.

GOOGLE / HAIRCUT PHOTOS
----------------------
Your existing haircut gallery remains fully editable in Pages CMS. When you add,
remove or reorder haircut photos there and Save, Cloudflare's Git-connected site
publishes the updated gallery automatically. This does NOT automatically import
photos that customers upload only to Google Maps.

The gallery now also has a 'VIEW OUR LATEST GOOGLE PHOTOS' link. That always opens
your business's live Google photo listing. If you want Google photos displayed
DIRECTLY inside your website and synced with Google, use an external photo widget.
For example, SociableKIT has a Google Business Photos widget:
https://www.sociablekit.com/free-widgets/free-google-business-photos-widget-website/
Copy its embed snippet into 'Optional Google Business Photos widget embed code'.
The free plan requires manual sync requests; automatic syncing is a paid feature.
No free fully automatic Google-photo sync is currently installed in this package.
